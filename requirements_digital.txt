# ── PDF geometry ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Issue #18 §3 needs exact per-line/span coordinates. Two candidates, and the choice
# is a LICENCE decision as much as a technical one — see the note below. Measured on
# tests/fixtures/digital/minimal.pdf, pdfplumber returns char-level geometry to three
# decimal places (x0=72.000 x1=80.004 top=62.484 bottom=74.484), plus fontname, size
# and the transformation matrix, and inter-line gaps that make block boundaries
# detectable (2.0 pt within a paragraph vs 34.0 pt between them).
pdfplumber    # MIT — char/word/line bboxes, font, size, matrix. Already a docmd-stack dep.
